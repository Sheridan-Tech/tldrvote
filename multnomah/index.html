Method, source provenance, transparency and limitations

Candidate names and offices are extracted from Multnomah County's Sept. 22 regular and ranked-choice ballot reports. Biographical fields come from candidate-submitted Oregon state or county pamphlet statements. The same comparison fields are presented for each candidate where available; absence of a field is not an adverse finding. Sources, partial external checks and material uncertainties are identified separately.

Official reports show 53 regular candidate-office listings and six distinct local measures, plus seven ranked-choice contests. The regular report header says 63 contests and 116 filed candidates, but the per-page official counts add to 60 contests and 109 candidates. Our extraction finds 53 standard office sections and six numbered local measures. The inconsistent report totals need clarification from the county; do not treat the header number or our index as a certified personalized ballot.

Each measure's yes/no and financial explanation is an assistant-prepared reading of the official ballot and linked public records—not an independent fiscal or legal audit. Outside reporting helps corroborate selected roles and quote positions, but does not prove promised results. No complete campaign finance, court/disciplines, legislative votes, professional license or fiscal forecasting audit is asserted.
