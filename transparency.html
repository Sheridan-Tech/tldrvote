Current publication status

Not certified as a complete, independently fact-checked election-information product. The material is source-linked for study and correction. It is not an official or personalized ballot, a government endorsement or a prediction of results.

Our reproducible release audit records 11 cleared checks and 6 unresolved checks. A passing technical check means its specified test passed—not that every factual claim is true.

Content inventory

Across the two editions: 111 topic screens, 206 candidate listings and 21 measures. Clark County includes 117 source-linked research findings from 69 public source pages. For Multnomah, 130 candidate-submitted pamphlet profiles passed programmatic name/heading checks, while 25 other candidate listings lack a reliably identified individual pamphlet profile. No lack of qualifications or policy positions is inferred from this absence.

Verified data fidelity versus unresolved questions

All four stored Multnomah original election PDFs match recorded SHA256 hashes. The 155 candidate ballot names were checked against the original report pages. Six Multnomah measure briefs passed selected official-text number checks and sample tax arithmetic. Clark candidate-statement text and dated source corrections were audited separately. These are provenance and transcription checks, not independent credential, finance, future-impact or allegation verdicts.

County report discrepancy: The official Multnomah E-019 report dated September 22 prints a final summary of 63 contests / 116 filings. Its detailed per-page totals add to 60 / 109. We have not received an explanation and will not silently adjust either number. The staged inventory includes 53 standard office listings, seven entries in the RCV report and six local measures. Official ballots and election rules

Personalized eligibility depends on a voter's address and precinct; this site does not ask for that address. Use Washington VoteWA or Oregon My Vote for the official personalized information. Original election pages: Clark County and Multnomah County.

Source and editorial rules

We distinguish official ballots and operative legal records from institutional biographies, agency forecasts, journalism, candidate self-reporting and advocacy. We identify who makes each assertion, its source date and what remains unknown. Recorded candidate statements are evidence of what was said, not proof that the assertion is correct. Forecasts are not guarantees. We do not rank candidates or recommend votes.

The project has a detailed internal editorial register and requires named human review before asserting that a material political claim has been fact-checked. This edition has no whole-candidate or whole-measure independent certification.

Corrections, ownership and privacy

Published independently by Sheridan Technologies; not affiliated with any election administrator, party, campaign or candidate. Corrections or requests for source reconsideration: [email protected]. Significant fixes are dated in the Clark County public changelog; do not rely on screenshots without dates.

This static preview does not intentionally collect or transmit a user's vote choices. Private notes in the Clark edition, where supported, are stored in the browser. A website hosting provider may process IP addresses, request logs and basic device data. Clicking third-party source links transfers your visit to those organizations; sending an email shares its contents with email providers. We have not established or claimed a zero-log hosting environment. Do not enter sensitive voter-registration details or private allegations into feedback emails.

Before significant promotion, legal review should determine whether any communication or sponsorship rules apply; nonpartisan educational intent alone does not settle advertising classification. See the Washington PDC guidance.
